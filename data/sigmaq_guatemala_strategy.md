# Sigmaq Guatemala - Strategic Opportunity Readout

## Executive conclusion

The message from Rafael is not a rejection. It is a phased purchasing signal.

The key interpretation is:

- Phase 1: yes, focus on waste handling and trim flow between corrugator and embaladora.
- Phase 2: bobbin logistics / paper reel handling is deferred and will require a separate discussion and budget.
- Phase 3: automated palletization and WIP/AMR automation is a later stage, not the initial commercial entry point.

This means the opportunity should be managed as a modular roadmap, not as a single all-inclusive project.

## Why this matters

The customer is clearly trying to reduce risk and sequence the investment. This is common in industrial automation sales. They want to first resolve the most visible pain point and validate technical fit before spending bigger amounts.

The first commercial win should therefore be the operationally obvious and ROI-visible bottleneck in waste / trim management.

## Commercial strategy

### Phase 1: win the first technical ROI case

Position the project around:

- waste handling
- trim flow efficiency
- process stability between corrugator and embaladora
- measurable reduction in material waste and operational friction

This is the most credible entry point because it is both tangible and easier to justify internally.

### Phase 2: expand into material logistics

Only after technical validation and proven ROI should we propose:

- bobbin logistics
- paper reel handling
- feed system to corrugator
- intralogistics between process areas

### Phase 3: palletization and broader automation

This remains part of the future roadmap but should not be presented as the first ask unless Sigmaq is clearly ready to budget it.

## Strategic recommendation

We should not force the full automation vision in one proposal. Instead, we should:

1. Accept the phase-gate structure.
2. Prepare a technical discussion around phase 1.
3. Sell the ROI and layout validation around waste + trim management.
4. Use the first success to open a second-stage conversation about bobbin logistics.
5. Keep palletization and AMR as a future roadmap item supported by data and performance proof.

## Risk to avoid

The main risk is trying to sell everything at once. That would make the proposal feel too broad, too expensive, and not aligned with the current budget logic.

The winning move is to make the first phase feel like a clear, low-risk, visible improvement.

## Recommended next steps

- Schedule a technical session focused on waste handling and trim flow.
- Prepare the concept of operations and layout review to compare current vs proposed flow.
- Develop a phased offer structure with Phase 1, Phase 2 and Phase 3 clearly separated.
- Keep a decision log and meeting notes in the repo to preserve context as the opportunity develops.
