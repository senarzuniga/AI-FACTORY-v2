Market Context and Opportunity

The project folder shows a concentrated opportunity centered on two DG VSOP 520 lines, each with a defined production configuration. These units are not presented as isolated machines, but as part of a broader production system designed around packaging conversion and web-fed process continuity.

The commercial logic is straightforward: buying a used or second-hand flexible web offset line may unlock production capacity with a lower capital impact than a new-line purchase, while still offering the performance, modularity and finishing integration needed for packaging applications.

Why this matters

  • Packaging and carton businesses frequently need flexible, high-volume capability.
  • Used press assets can reduce capital exposure and speed up capacity deployment.
  • Modular systems improve the ability to serve multiple formats and customers.

Commercial objective

  • Reduce initial capex without losing production capability.
  • Accelerate implementation and commissioning.
  • Build a production platform with finishing integration already considered.
03

Equipment Profile

Two configurations are described in the documentation and they share a common design language and production logic:

Configuration A: 7-color + flexo + die-cutter

  • VSOP 520 platform with variable sleeve offset process.
  • Approximately 20.5 inch maximum web width.
  • Material compatibility spanning paper and paperboard.
  • Maximum print repeat of 30 inches.
  • 7 offset print units and 1 flexo unit with enclosed ink chamber.
  • Martin unwind, corona treatment, UV drying, web guides, cleaner, and roller control systems.
  • Direct integration with Zerand platen die-cutter and delivery table.
  • Tooling inventory, plate sleeves, blanket sleeves, plate trimmer, and mounting equipment.

Configuration B: 5-color + flexo + die-cutter

  • VSOP 520 with optimized packaging configuration.
  • 5 offset units plus 1 flexo unit.
  • Integrated drying, web control, register control, and ink management systems.
  • New 2020 BST web viewing system and closed-loop color control.
  • Five complete sets of new rollers.
  • Strong fit for short-to-medium run packaging and conversion activities.

Risk Controls and Due Diligence

The documentation provides a strong technical foundation, but the buyer still needs to validate actual condition before closing. A sensible de-risking process should include physical inspection, service history review, run-hour review, and functional testing.
