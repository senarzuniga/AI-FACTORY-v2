03 · Riesgo que debe cerrar la auditoria

Un conveyor rapido no corrige una secuencia lenta.

Riesgo 1

Cuello desplazado

La nueva salida evacua mas rapido, pero flejado, retractilado, remontado, control de calidad o pickup saturan a continuacion.

Riesgo 2

Capacidad desconectada

Equipos dimensionados por velocidad media fallan en picos, bundles grandes, pallets incompletos, excepciones o cambio de receta.

Riesgo 3

Islas de control

PLC y drives capaces de automatizar permanecen aislados; las decisiones de prioridad, destino y recuperacion siguen siendo manuales.

Riesgo 4

Buffer mal entendido

Mas acumulacion puede ocultar el problema, aumentar WIP y retrasar la deteccion de una salida detenida.

Riesgo 5

Disponibilidad comun

Una arquitectura compartida sin bypass ni modo degradado puede convertir una averia local en parada de varias convertidoras.

Riesgo 6

Dato no reconciliado

Cajas/h, bundles/h, pallets/h y ft2/h no son intercambiables sin receta, conteo, patron y mix.

Pregunta de decision¿Que combinacion de capacidad, buffer, redundancia y software mantiene cada convertidora por debajo del limite de bloqueo aceptable durante picos y fallos previsibles?
03 · Risk the audit must close

A fast conveyor does not correct a slow sequence.

Risk 1

Shifted bottleneck

The new outfeed clears faster, but strapping, wrapping, restacking, quality control or pickup saturates next.

Risk 2

Disconnected capacity

Equipment sized on average rate fails under peaks, large bundles, incomplete pallets, exceptions or recipe changes.

Risk 3

Control islands

PLCs and drives that could be automated remain isolated; priority, destination and recovery decisions stay manual.

Risk 4

Misread buffer

More accumulation may hide the problem, increase WIP and delay detection of a stopped downstream process.

Risk 5

Common availability

A shared architecture without bypass or degraded mode can turn one local fault into several stopped converters.

Risk 6

Unreconciled units

Boxes/h, bundles/h, pallets/h and ft2/h are not interchangeable without recipe, count, pattern and mix.

Decision questionWhich combination of capacity, buffer, redundancy and software keeps each converter below its acceptable blocking limit during foreseeable peaks and failures?
04 · Modelo de capacidad

Dimensionar por evento, simultaneidad y percentil.

Output bueno restringido = min(convertidora, bundler, remontador, insertador, flejadora, retractiladora, conveyor, pickup) × disponibilidad del sistema
Cajas recuperadas = minutos de bloqueo evitados × cajas buenas/min sostenidas
Valor anual = cajas recuperadas × margen de contribucion unitario - OPEX incremental

Ejemplo transparente: una EVOL

A 24.000 cajas/h teoricas, cada minuto bloqueado equivale como maximo a 400 cajas no producidas. Evitar solo 15 min de bloqueo por turno representa hasta 6.000 cajas por turno antes de aplicar mix, calidad y disponibilidad.

Ejemplo de sensibilidad, no promesa de output.

Escala de las tres EVOL

A pico teorico combinado, un minuto comun de restriccion equivale como maximo a 1.200 cajas. La auditoria sustituira ese maximo por velocidad buena observada y minutos clasificados por causa.

04 · Capacity model

Size by event, concurrency and percentile.

Constrained good output = min(converter, bundler, restacker, pallet inserter, strapper, wrapper, conveyor, pickup) × system availability
Recovered boxes = avoided blocked minutes × sustained good boxes/min
Annual value = recovered boxes × unit contribution margin - incremental OPEX

Transparent example: one EVOL

At a theoretical 24,000 boxes/h, each blocked minute represents at most 400 unproduced boxes. Avoiding only 15 blocked minutes per shift represents up to 6,000 boxes per shift before applying mix, quality and availability.

Sensitivity example, not an output commitment.

Scale of three EVOLs

At combined theoretical peak, one common constrained minute represents at most 1,200 boxes. The audit will replace that maximum with observed good speed and cause-coded minutes.

05 · Flejado y formacion de carga

Tres lineas no son una validacion de capacidad.

La suficiencia del flejado debe demostrarse contra la demanda p95 y el mix real, incluyendo colocacion de cantoneras, doble fleje, cambios, rechazos y disponibilidad. La hipotesis de trabajo es evaluar una cuarta linea o sustituir tecnologia actual por flejadoras automaticas con insercion automatica de cantoneras.

N flejadoras requeridas = techo[demanda p95 de cargas/min ÷ (capacidad sostenible por flejadora × disponibilidad)] + redundancia requerida
Situacion

3 lineas cuestionadas

La experiencia operativa indica insuficiencia. Debe documentarse por colas, blocked time, utilizacion y recuperacion tras fallo.

Alternativa A

Cuarta flejadora

Aumenta capacidad y redundancia si routing, espacio, operarios y pickup permiten distribuir la carga.

Alternativa B

Automatica + cantoneras

Elimina tareas manuales del ciclo critico y puede elevar fuertemente el ritmo sostenible del puesto final.

Interpretacion correcta del 400-600%Una mejora neta del 400-600% significa que el nuevo puesto produciria entre 5 y 7 veces el output base, si “mejora” se usa en sentido incremental. La auditoria fijara la convencion, cronometrara ciclo actual y automatico por SKU y evitara convertir una afirmacion comercial en garantia sin FAT/SAT.

05 · Strapping and load formation

Three lines are not a capacity validation.

Strapping sufficiency must be proven against p95 demand and actual mix, including corner-board placement, double straps, changeovers, rejects and availability. The working hypothesis is to assess a fourth line or replace current technology with automatic strappers and automatic corner-board insertion.

Required strappers = ceiling[p95 load demand/min ÷ (sustainable capacity per strapper × availability)] + required redundancy
Situation

3 lines challenged

Operating experience indicates insufficient capacity. This must be documented through queues, blocked time, utilization and failure recovery.

Option A

Fourth strapper

Adds capacity and redundancy if routing, space, labor and pickup can distribute the load.

Option B

Automatic + corner boards

Removes manual tasks from the critical cycle and can sharply increase the final station's sustainable rate.

Correct interpretation of 400-600%A net 400-600% improvement means the new station would produce 5 to 7 times baseline output if “improvement” is incremental. The audit will define the convention, time current and automatic cycles by SKU, and prevent a commercial claim from becoming a guarantee without FAT/SAT.

06 · Ejecucion en planta

Tres dias, cuatro disciplinas, un solo modelo de flujo.

Dia 1 · Gemba, baseline y taxonomia de perdidas

Mapear rutas y estados; cronometrar ciclos; capturar formatos, bundles y pallets; identificar starvation, blocking, microparadas, rehandling, esperas de operador y acumulacion. Alinear tags y causas con produccion, mantenimiento y calidad.

Dia 2 · Electricidad, PLC, drives y conectividad

Inventariar PLC/HMI/VFD/servo, versiones, redes y protocolos; revisar I/O, handshakes, safety, alarmas, historicos y capacidad de datos; comprobar interfaces de paquetadoras, remontadoras, insertadores, flejadoras, wrappers y conveyors.

Dia 3 · Stress test, quick wins y arquitectura objetivo

Observar picos y cambios; probar recuperacion y modos manuales acordados; validar colas y restricciones; priorizar acciones inmediatas; cerrar requisitos funcionales, datos faltantes y pruebas de aceptacion de la inversion.

Equipo INGECART

Direccion tecnica, direccion de ingenieria, ingenieria electrica y software, y direccion de proyecto. Participacion coordinada con produccion, mantenimiento, IT/OT, calidad y seguridad de PCG.

Metodo de captura

Observacion directa, time study, export de historicos, conteo de eventos, entrevistas estructuradas, revision de planos y programas disponibles, fotografia tecnica autorizada y reconciliacion al final de cada jornada.

06 · On-site execution

Three days, four disciplines, one flow model.

Day 1 · Gemba, baseline and loss taxonomy

Map routes and states; time cycles; capture formats, bundles and pallets; identify starvation, blocking, microstops, rehandling, operator waits and accumulation. Align tags and causes with production, maintenance and quality.

Day 2 · Electrical, PLC, drives and connectivity

Inventory PLC/HMI/VFD/servo assets, versions, networks and protocols; review I/O, handshakes, safety, alarms, histories and data capability; verify interfaces across bundlers, restackers, pallet inserters, strappers, wrappers and conveyors.

Day 3 · Stress test, quick wins and target architecture

Observe peaks and changeovers; test agreed recovery and manual modes; validate queues and constraints; prioritize immediate actions; close functional requirements, missing data and investment acceptance tests.

INGECART team

Technical direction, engineering direction, electrical and software engineering, and project direction. Coordinated participation with PCG production, maintenance, IT/OT, quality and safety.

Capture method

Direct observation, time study, history exports, event counts, structured interviews, available drawing and program review, authorized technical photography and end-of-day reconciliation.

11 · Decision gates

The investment advances when the system closes.

  • PCG-approved blocked/starved-time baseline.
  • SKU-bundle-pallet-destination matrix covers critical volume and families.
  • p95 capacity demonstrated at every operation and shared route.
  • Strapping meets demand with defined availability and redundancy.
  • Pallet inserters validated against peak consumption and replenishment.
  • Bypass and degraded mode preserve safe, traceable production.
  • PLC, drives and WCS have defined interfaces, ownership and recovery.
  • FAT/SAT reproduces realistic mix, peaks, exceptions and restart.
  • Implemented quick wins are not negated by the new design.
  • Benefits use good output and contribution margin without double counting.
RecommendationAuthorize the audit as a pre-engineering gate. Freeze the final architecture only after reconciling capacity, concurrency, integration and contingencies with Middletown data.
