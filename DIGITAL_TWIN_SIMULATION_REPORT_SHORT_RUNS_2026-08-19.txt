DIGITAL TWIN SIMULATION REPORT
Benchmark Simulation – Forklift Logistics vs. INGETRANS Automated Reel Feeding
High-Mix, Short-Run Corrugated Plant Scenario

1. Executive Summary

This scenario tests the same logistics logic under a much more demanding operating pattern:

- Frequent reel changes
- High SKU mix
- Short production runs
- High setup frequency
- Tight delivery windows
- Greater sensitivity to waiting for the next reel

Two scenarios are compared under identical conditions:

- Scenario A – Conventional forklift-based reel handling
- Scenario B – INGETRANS automated reel supply and return system

The objective is to evaluate how logistics continuity affects performance when the plant is dominated by short runs rather than long, stable production sequences.

The result is clear: in short-run environments the forklift system suffers disproportionately because the process loses time not in the corrugator itself, but in waiting for the next reel, repositioning loads, searching for the correct material and resetting the line after frequent disruptions.

5. Production Continuity in Short-Run Work

The short-run scenario makes the problem much more severe.

Under long-running jobs, a forklift system can sometimes absorb small disruptions. In short-run environments, the time lost is not just a few minutes; it is the loss of the next product window, the pre-setup gap, the waiting time for material and the sequential impact at the machine.

The Digital Twin shows that the gain does not come from increasing machine speed. Both scenarios operate at the same corrugator speed. The gain comes from keeping the next reel ready at the right stand at the right moment.

This is where INGETRANS produces a disproportionate advantage:

- lower reel waiting time,
- higher readiness at each stand,
- less operator intervention,
- much lower congestion close to the corrugator,
- smoother transitions between short jobs,
- lower risk of starving the line during frequent changeovers.

10. Why INGETRANS Improves Performance in Short Runs

The main reason is not machine speed. It is the ability to maintain reel continuity under high changeover stress.

The forklift system is especially penalised by:
- waiting for forklift availability,
- moving loads around the corrugator,
- searching for the correct reel,
- congestion near the machine,
- dependency on operator coordination,
- disruption caused by frequent layout changes and multiple short lots.

INGETRANS reduces this by:
- continuous reel availability,
- automatic reel replenishment,
- automatic return and sequencing,
- reduced congestion around the production line,
- lower operator intervention,
- tighter synchronization between reel consumption and supply,
- more stable transitions between jobs.

In short-run production, the system does not just move paper more efficiently; it preserves the operating rhythm of the corrugator.

12. Conclusion

The short-run scenario confirms a key industrial truth:

In a high-mix, short-run production environment, reel logistics is not a support function. It becomes a production-critical system.

The benchmark shows that:

- Production gain: +12.8%
- OEE gain: +8 points
- Starvation reduction: -86%
- Logistics-related downtime reduction: -75%+
- Additional capacity: more than 7 million metres per year
- Operating savings: over €360,000 per year

For short jobs, the gap between forklift logistics and INGETRANS is not just visible in pure logistics KPIs; it becomes visible in schedule adherence, utilisation, and output delivered.

This is exactly the scenario where the value of automated reel feeding is strongest: when the problem is no longer “how fast can the machine run,” but “how consistently can the next reel be delivered before the machine stops waiting.”

Executive Recommendation

For a plant dominated by short production runs, the preferred design is not simply “more capacity in the machine.” It is a logistics architecture that guarantees reel continuity at the point of use.

That is the real strategic advantage of INGETRANS:
- less waiting,
- less dependence on manual logistics,
- lower starvation risk,
